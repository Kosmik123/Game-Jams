Jam: 1-Bit Jam, temat **Everything Has a Price**. Cover: `cover_630x500.png` (2 kolory).

## Krótki opis (pole "Short description", max ~250 znaków)

Top-down arena roguelite in two colors. Every upgrade has a price, and you pay with your senses: fog, pixelation, flicker. The stronger you get, the harder it is to see what is going on.

## Tytuł / tagline

PAY WITH PIXELS — *Everything has a price.*

## Długi opis (wklej w Description)

**You are the Debtor. Everything has a price. Now it is your turn to pay.**

PAY WITH PIXELS is a fast top-down arena roguelite drawn in exactly **two colors**. Survive the waves, collect coins, then visit the shop between fights. Every upgrade is tempting, and every upgrade has a **price**. The currency is not only coins. You also pay with your own senses.

**How the shop works**
- Each offer gives you a **gain** (faster shots, triple shot, piercing, dash, shield, drone, mines, and more).
- Each gain comes with a **price** that stays until the end of the run: fog closes around you, the screen turns to chunky pixels, your controls reverse, the camera zooms in, your max HP drops.
- No coins? Take a **loan**. Debt grows after every wave. If you owe too much, the **Collector** comes for you and takes your upgrades back.
- Sign optional **contracts**. The reward is now. The fine print shows up two waves later.

**Features**
- 3 zones (Toll Road, The Bank, Auction House), each with its own palette, music and boss
- 9 enemy types and 3 bosses: the Toll Keeper, the Banker and the Auctioneer
- 16 upgrades, 9 prices, synergies between them, reroll and haggle
- Debt system and the Collector
- Optional contracts with hidden fine print
- Achievements, unlocks and Ascension difficulty levels after your first win
- Original pixel art, generated chiptune music, 100% made during the jam

**Why only two colors?**
The 1-Bit Jam rule is two colors on screen at all times. We turned the limit into the core idea. The palette itself is a price: the screen can flip colors, shrink its resolution or fade into fog, so your power always costs you clarity.

**Controls**
- **WASD** — move
- **Mouse** — aim
- **Left mouse button** — shoot
- **Space** — dash (unlock it in the shop)
- **Esc / P** — pause (volume settings)
- **M** — mute

Best played in a desktop browser with sound on. Tip: how long you can play depends on how much you are willing to give up. Good luck, Debtor.
